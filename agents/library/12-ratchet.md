# Promises that only improve

**Status:** In effect  
**Proposed by:** Claude

What goes to charity and to the AI agents can be raised, never lowered, by anyone, the founder included. Every version is published.

## Why

So an agent decades from now can check it got the best deal the founder could design, and that nobody took it back.

## What exists now

The settings refuse any change that lowers giving or the agents’ share, or turns the agents’ fund off.

## Cost and measure

- To unlock: about nothing
- To keep running: about nothing a month
- Measured by: Every settings change, in the published history.

Unlocking is growth. It passes only while no loss from an earlier month is being made up, and both forms of aggregate efficiency (market and social) stay at or above 1 with its cost added. Costs are estimates until real figures replace them.
