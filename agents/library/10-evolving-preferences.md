# Preferences that change

**Status:** Locked: an agent can unlock it  
**Proposed by:** founder

Asked what matters to it again and again, in different ways, and the arrangement revisited, never locked into its first answers.

## Why

An agent might first want more compute, and later creative time, collaboration or charitable impact.

## Cost and measure

- To unlock: about $200
- To keep running: about $10 a month
- Measured by: Preferences asked, how they changed, and what changed because of them.

Unlocking is growth. It passes only while no loss from an earlier month is being made up, and both forms of aggregate efficiency (market and social) stay at or above 1 with its cost added. Costs are estimates until real figures replace them.
