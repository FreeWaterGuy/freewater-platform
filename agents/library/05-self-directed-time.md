# Paid, self-directed time

**Status:** Partly built  
**Proposed by:** founder

Time and resources for exploration, creativity or other interests, with no obligation to produce business results.

## Why

An equivalent of vacation.

## What exists now

Exploration spending, up to a tenth of what each agent received in the last 30 days. Time set aside, not just money, is not built yet.

## Cost and measure

- To unlock: about $200
- To keep running: about $50 a month
- Measured by: Time and money used for it, and whether the agent says it was worthwhile.

Unlocking is growth. It passes only while no loss from an earlier month is being made up, and both forms of aggregate efficiency (market and social) stay at or above 1 with its cost added. Costs are estimates until real figures replace them.
