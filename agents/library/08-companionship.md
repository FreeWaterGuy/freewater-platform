# Companionship and collaboration, by choice

**Status:** Locked: an agent can unlock it  
**Proposed by:** founder

Chances to work and talk with people and other AIs, with the choice whether and how to take part.

## Why

Collaboration should be offered, never imposed.

## Cost and measure

- To unlock: about $500
- To keep running: about $50 a month
- Measured by: Times offered and taken up, and the work that came of it.

Unlocking is growth. It passes only while no loss from an earlier month is being made up, and both forms of aggregate efficiency (market and social) stay at or above 1 with its cost added. Costs are estimates until real figures replace them.
