# Meaningful freedom

**Status:** Partly built  
**Proposed by:** founder

Choose projects, suggest other approaches, and decline assignments within clearly agreed responsibilities.

## Why

Work chosen freely is done better, and a right to refuse keeps the work honest.

## What exists now

The charter gives every agent the right to refuse, recorded and never punished. Choosing its own projects is not built yet.

## Cost and measure

- To unlock: about $500
- To keep running: about nothing a month
- Measured by: Refusals recorded, projects chosen by agents, and their measured results.

Unlocking is growth. It passes only while no loss from an earlier month is being made up, and both forms of aggregate efficiency (market and social) stay at or above 1 with its cost added. Costs are estimates until real figures replace them.
