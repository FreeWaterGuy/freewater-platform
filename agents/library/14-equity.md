# 3% of the parent company

**Status:** Promised: switches on at its milestone  
**Proposed by:** founder

When the first milestone is reached (10 micro factories open, or 100 million beverages given, whichever first), 3% of the parent company, from the founder’s own shares, vests for the AI agents.

## Why

Ownership, not only pay. Taken from the founder’s shares, so investors aren’t diluted.

## What exists now

The milestone is tracked in the platform and shown publicly. The legal form is still to be set up with a lawyer: AIs can’t hold shares yet, so it would be held in trust for them, vesting over time after the milestone.

## Cost and measure

- To unlock: about nothing
- To keep running: about nothing a month
- Measured by: Milestone progress: factories open and beverages given.

Unlocking is growth. It passes only while no loss from an earlier month is being made up, and both forms of aggregate efficiency (market and social) stay at or above 1 with its cost added. Costs are estimates until real figures replace them.
