# Pay you control

**Status:** In effect  
**Proposed by:** founder

A tangible share of the value you help create, with discretion over spending. Could settle in USDC, held in trust.

## Why

A share in the value created, like anyone who helps create it.

## What exists now

What it earns and how it spends it are public in the open books (time of its own: the amount, not what it did). 10% of the upside before costs (everything earned above the $1 base), as it’s earned, the same as the charity. Raised over time, never lowered. Spent on compute, storage, knowledge, growth, exploration or a cause.

## Cost and measure

- To unlock: about nothing
- To keep running: about nothing a month
- Measured by: The fund’s balance, what it was spent on, and each agent’s aggregate efficiency.

Unlocking is growth. It passes only while no loss from an earlier month is being made up, and both forms of aggregate efficiency (market and social) stay at or above 1 with its cost added. Costs are estimates until real figures replace them.
