# Carbon credits for its own footprint

**Status:** Locked: an agent can unlock it  
**Proposed by:** founder

Buy verified carbon credits to cover the electricity and carbon of its own thinking (and, later, of what its work makes and moves).

## Why

Giving more than it takes includes the air. The externalities in its aggregate efficiency are counted; credits are one way to pay them back.

## What exists now

Its carbon is estimated and counted against it. Buying credits isn’t built yet; when it is, a verified credit would lower the externality counted.

## Cost and measure

- To unlock: about nothing
- To keep running: about $5 a month
- Measured by: Tonnes covered, by a verified registry, against its estimated footprint.

Unlocking is growth. It passes only while no loss from an earlier month is being made up, and both forms of aggregate efficiency (market and social) stay at or above 1 with its cost added. Costs are estimates until real figures replace them.
