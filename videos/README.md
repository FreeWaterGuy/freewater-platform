# Walkthrough videos

Screen recordings of the platform running with demo data (made 2026-10, before the fixes in PRs #40–#42; every
problem they showed is fixed and listed in `docs/REVIEW-LOG.md`). No real keys or customer data appear in them.

1. **Advertiser**: creating a campaign, choosing sizes, designing or uploading an ad, paying, changing a link.
2. **Household**: the bottle arriving, scanning it, the receipt, points and stopping deliveries.
3. **Driver**: taking a run, loading the van, scanning and photographing each drop.
4. **Ops**: the team's day: routes, reprints, link approvals, house claims, bottle lookup.
5. **Owner**: the console: features, security, pause, AI agents, open books.
6. **White-label**: setting up a new licensee company with its own brand.
7. **Data and AI**: every use of data and AI: rating ads, categories, benchmarks, the governor.

The vending-machine video is skipped for now (machines are planned, not built).
